How it works

  1. Build your buylist. Select each game, choose its condition, and enter the quantity.
  2. Submit your details. Enter your contact email and PayPal email.
  3. Print your invoice. After submitting, the invoice will show your item list and the ship-to address. After submitting, message us on Discord at DulacDat or text 985-240-9339. Tell us your name and when your package is expected to arrive.
  4. Pack and ship. Put your games in a secure box and include a printed copy of the invoice. You buy your own shipping label and send the box to the address printed on the invoice.
  5. Inspection and payout. Games are checked on arrival. Final payout is confirmed after inspection and may change if the items or conditions differ from your submission.

Condition guidelines

Games should be in approximately 9/10 condition and free of major damage. Normal light wear is okay. Disc resurfacing is acceptable as long as the disc is repairable and plays properly. Please do not list cracked, badly damaged, or unplayable items as standard-condition games.
